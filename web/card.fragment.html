A Card in done is no one's to let go of - it is not draggable and its claim only says who did the work - so it wears neither the edge nor the name here. A Card in closed is the same case for the same reason, and a stronger one: the only way out of that Column is reopening, so a drag would be a Move the Board refuses. movable in gate.go says which Cards those two are, once for the edge, the drag and the name, because it is the same fact the Board's Column gate refuses a move out of those Columns with. A Card an Agent filed that nobody has opened since carries a light-blue asterisk before its title, in every Column: that is the board saying which Cards arrived since the human last looked, and opening the Card is what clears it - not age, not a move. A held Card wears the asterisk and the orange edge at once, because being new and being worked on are two different facts.
